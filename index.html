Character Profile: Claire Ashchoo

Home | Classified Files

Introduction

Welcome, Muggle! This is Claire Ashchoo, the Prefect of Slytherin.

During her last year at Hogwarts, her mission is to mingling with Muggle students and writing reports on them.

So, you might be able to find her studying at Toronto Metropolitan University, somewhere, maybe?

Origin Story

Born into a prominent though relatively young—lineage of wizards in Bristol, Claire Ashchoo is much like a rose with thorns, carefully sheltered by her mother. The Ashchoo clan has never allowed her to lift a finger for any strenuous task; by all appearances, she has lived the life of a pampered little princess.

Yet, one should not mistake this for a life of carefree indulgence. Even her "free" time is strictly regimented, mapped out by her father, Mr. Ashchoo. With the pragmatic mindset of a port-city merchant, he has ensured his daughter masters the violin and piano, alongside French and Spanish—all in preparation for her to eventually inherit the family’s vast fortune. He has even gone so far as to arrange a betrothal for her, purely to secure a new business contract, an act that has left Claire harboring a deep, burning resentment toward him for treating her as nothing more than a bargaining chip. And yet, in a cruel twist of irony, there remains a part of her that is strikingly like him.
