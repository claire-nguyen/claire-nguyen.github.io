Fun Facts

  • She has a whole shelf of perfumes, but she has worn only one scent for years: magnolia and bluebell.
  • She can guess every spice in a soup on the first sip, even if she hates cooking.
  • She always choose the seat facing the door and hate having her back wide open to a room.

Survival Guide for Working With Me

  1. Do not wait for her to say hello first. She never will, so introduce yourself.
  2. Come prepared and make decisions quickly. She has no patience for people who fiddle with every detail or run away from a challenge.
  3. Earn her trust slowly and patiently. Once you have it, you will have a friend who fights for you (if you are Slytherin, of course!!!).
  4. Do not serve her anything sour or bitter, but passion fruit mousse works!

Strengths

  • Particularly gifted in Charms, Defense Against the Dark Arts, and Potions, which supports her in an excellent performance in Chemistry within the Muggle world.
  • Possesses keen observational skills, allowing her to replicate tasks or learn new things after seeing them done just once.

Weaknesses

Even the most cunning Slytherin has a weakness.

  • Her deepest fear is being faded or ignored, and it drives her to overwork and push herself to exhaustion.
  • Quite poor at Care of Magical Creatures, as she lacks any real interest in animals.
  • She judges silently and trusts slowly, which makes friendships hard to start.

Future Ambitions

Claire Ashchoo want to finish her studies at the top of her class, then rise to a position of real influence in the wizarding world. She wants power, but not for its own sake. She wants a name that stands on its own, not in the shadow of the Ashmore name. Above all, she wants to prove, to herself first, that she can never be forgotten.
